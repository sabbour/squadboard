## Prototype brief

- Hypothesis to validate
- Target persona and scenario
- Critical path flow
- Screens, artifacts, or clickable interactions needed
- Sample data or content needed
- Questions for reviewers or users
- Success signal and decision threshold

## Guardrails

Prototype the smallest useful slice. Include empty states, error states, and handoff notes when they affect the decision.
