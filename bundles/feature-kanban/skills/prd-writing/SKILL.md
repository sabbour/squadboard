## PRD sections

1. Summary
2. Customer problem
3. Target users and scenarios
4. Goals and non-goals
5. Requirements and acceptance criteria
6. User experience notes
7. Metrics and instrumentation
8. Risks, dependencies, and mitigations
9. Rollout and rollback plan
10. Open questions

## Quality bar

Every requirement must be testable. Every goal must tie to a measurable outcome. Non-goals must be explicit enough to prevent scope creep.
