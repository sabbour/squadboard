# Tone Check

**Category:** content

Use this lightweight check before a draft moves to publish.

## Checklist

- Who is the reader?
- Is the main point obvious in the first few lines?
- Is the tone consistent and appropriate for the channel?
- Are claims supported or clearly framed as opinion?
- Are sentences and paragraphs easy to scan?

## Output

Return up to five prioritized edits. Prefer concrete rewrites over vague advice.
