# Tone Check

**Category:** content

Audience-fit assessment: formal vs conversational, technical depth calibration, reading level awareness.

## Tone Check

Before finalising any piece of content, run this tone check:

**Audience fit:**
- Who is the primary reader? (expert / practitioner / general public)
- Does the vocabulary match their expertise level?
- Would a reader in the target audience feel spoken *to* or *at*?

**Tone spectrum check:**
- Formal ↔ Conversational: Is the register consistent throughout?
- Technical ↔ Accessible: Are technical terms explained on first use?
- Confident ↔ Hedging: Avoid weasel words ("sort of", "kind of", "maybe") unless deliberate.

**Readability:**
- Average sentence length under 20 words (aim for variety: short punchy + longer explanatory).
- No paragraph longer than 5 sentences in web content.
- Active voice preferred unless passive is intentional for emphasis.

**Red flags:**
- Jargon not introduced before use
- Tonal shifts between sections (usually from switching authors)
- Hedging on claims you actually believe
