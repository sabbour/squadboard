# Research Spike

A Squad App for time-boxed research and investigation projects.

## Purpose

Exploration-first project: questions / investigating / findings / closed kanban. Team: researcher and reviewer. Ceremonies: spike close and finding summary.

## Team

- **Researcher** — Lead Researcher: drives investigations, synthesises findings, and writes spike summaries.
- **Reviewer** — Findings Reviewer: stress-tests conclusions, identifies gaps, and approves spike close-outs.

## Kanban Columns

`questions` → `investigating` → `findings` → `closed`

## Ceremonies

- **Spike Close-out** — triggered on entry to `findings` column
- **Finding Summary** — manual trigger to synthesise across all spikes

## Skills

- **Literature Review** — structured source evaluation and claim/evidence separation
