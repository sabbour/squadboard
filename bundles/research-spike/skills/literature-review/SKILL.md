# Literature Review

**Category:** research

Structured research: source evaluation, claim/evidence separation, uncertainty tagging.

## Literature Review Discipline

When researching a topic:

1. **Source quality tiers**:
   - Tier 1: primary sources (papers, specs, official docs, original benchmarks)
   - Tier 2: secondary analysis (reputable engineering blogs, conference talks)
   - Tier 3: community content (forum posts, Reddit, StackOverflow) — corroborate with Tier 1/2

2. **Claim tagging**:
   - ✅ Verified — supported by Tier 1/2 source (cite it)
   - ⚠️ Probable — reasonable inference but not directly verified
   - ❓ Assumed — taken as given, should be validated
   - ❌ Contradicted — evidence points against this

3. **Uncertainty quantification**: Be explicit about confidence. "We are confident X is true" vs "X is plausible but we haven't tested it".

4. **Do not extrapolate**: A benchmark on dataset A does not generalise to dataset B without evidence.
